1. Data We Collect

When you create an account we collect your name, email address and a password, which is stored in hashed form. When you use Waffle Wallet we also store the expense details you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to operate Waffle Wallet: to sign you in, to save and display your expenses, and to produce the summaries and filters you request. We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the application's database and is accessible only through your account. We take reasonable measures to protect it, but no system is completely secure. You can edit or delete your expenses at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Waffle Wallet loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when a page loads. We do not share your expense data with any third party. If this changes, we will update this policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at support@wafflewallet.example.
